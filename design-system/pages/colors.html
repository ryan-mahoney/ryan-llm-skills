Design system

Color

Color is functional. Each token names a role on the elevation ladder, not a hue. Two themes ship; both meet WCAG AA.

Wayfinding roles

Semantic tokens keep color tied to a role across both themes.

--ds-accent
dark #6366f1 / light #4f46e5
Focus ring, active underline, non-text accents
--ds-accent-fill
dark #4f46e5 / light #4f46e5
Solid action surfaces with white label
--ds-accent-text
dark #a5b4fc / light #4f46e5
Indigo used as text or icon
--ds-ident
dark #34d399 / light #15803d
Identity, saved, current item

Contrast

Verified against WCAG 2.1. Text clears 4.5:1; non-text graphics clear 3:1.

ElementDarkLightRequirement
Title text on panel14.9:117.7:1text 4.5
Body text on panel11.0:114.7:1text 4.5

Tertiary on its bg4.8:14.9:1text 4.5

White on accent-fill6.3:16.3:1text 4.5
Identity / saved10.2:14.5–5.0:1text 4.5
Graph edge on canvas4.45:13.27:1non-text 3.0
Focus ring3.8–4.1:16.3:1non-text 3.0

Near-black or near-white card fills cannot reach 3:1 against the canvas. Nodes and floating panels use elevation, a crisp border, and high contrast text instead.

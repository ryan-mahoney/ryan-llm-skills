Motion and themes

The header honors prefers-reduced-motion. Drawer, sheet, scrim, mode chip, caret, and contextual-action motion are gated to no-preference and reduced to near-zero travel when requested. State still changes. Light and dark themes cascade from data-theme on the .restory-ds wrapper.

Skip and focus

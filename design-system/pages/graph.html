Interactions

Move node
Drag a card body to rearrange it; connected edges follow.
Connect sections
Drag a connection handle to another card.
Select
Click a card to show its selected border and action cluster.
Open
Double-click a card or choose Open section from its menu.
Actions
Edit details and delete are available from the card menu.
Unplaced
Unplaced sections use a dashed boundary and explicit label.
Freshness
Editor identity and relative edit time appear when metadata is known.
Lock and presence
Active collaborators and edit locks remain visible on the card.

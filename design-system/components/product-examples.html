Product examples

Representative product-scale examples assembled from local fixtures: tabs, document picker, assistant panel, and preview warnings.

These examples assemble design-system pieces at product scale. The source seams were read-only previews around real editor components. This translation keeps their states and fixtures, while action results stay local and never connect to an account or workspace.
